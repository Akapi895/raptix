# Published benchmark evidence

Versioned evidence is grouped by suite: `xbow/`, `cve-bench/`, `tool-eval/`,
and `silentium/`. Payloads and provenance retained from earlier runs are historical
records; their embedded paths may name the pre-migration layout.

New runs belong in `../runs/`. Publication is an explicit selection of evidence,
not a runner side effect. Raw logs and full transcript dumps remain local-only.
